# Talking Dog Video Script Templates

Use these as starting points. Keep each finished script around 70-95 words for a 30-45 second video.

## Birthday

Hi [recipient name], it's me, [dog name]. I heard it is your birthday, so I had to stop everything, even my very important nap schedule, to say happy birthday. You are one of my favorite humans because [special detail]. I hope your day is full of treats, belly laughs, and people telling you how wonderful you are. If there is cake, please remember that I am available for cleanup duty. Happy birthday from your favorite pup, [dog name].

## Thank You

Hi [recipient name], it's [dog name]. I wanted to say thank you in my most official dog voice. Thank you for [reason]. It means so much, and if I could send you a bouquet, it would probably be made of treats and tennis balls. You bring so much joy to the people around you, and I hope this little message makes you smile. Sending tail wags, happy paws, and a very grateful doggy kiss.

## Get Well Soon

Hi [recipient name], it's [dog name]. I heard you could use a little extra love, so I am sending you my best healing tail wags. Please rest, take it easy, and remember that everyone is cheering for you. If I were there, I would curl up beside you, supervise your snacks, and make sure you got plenty of cozy time. Feel better soon. You are loved more than you know.

## Just Because

Hi [recipient name], it's me, [dog name]. There is no big reason for this message except that you are loved, appreciated, and honestly pretty great. I wanted to remind you that you make life brighter for the people around you. Also, if you ever need someone to celebrate you, I am available with tail wags and dramatic enthusiasm. Hope this made you smile. Now please go do something nice for yourself.

## Funny Dog Mom Message

Hi Mom, it's [dog name]. I just wanted to say thank you for being my favorite human, my snack manager, my door opener, my blanket provider, and my personal assistant. I know I can be a little [personality], but that is only because I am full of charm. Thank you for the treats, the belly rubs, and the love. You make my tail wag every single day, and I think you are pawsitively the best.

## Light Roast

Hi [recipient name], it's [dog name]. I was told to be nice, so I will only say that you are one of my favorite humans, even if [funny habit or joke]. Honestly, I admire your confidence. You bring so much fun and love to everyone around you, and I guess that is why we keep you. I hope this message makes you laugh, because you deserve it. Now please reward yourself with a treat. I know I would.
