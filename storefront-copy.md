## About Section

I started this shop because dog people know the truth: a message from the dog is almost always better than a regular card.

Each video is made from your dog's photo and the details you share in the order form. You tell me who the video is for, the occasion, the dog's personality, and any special details to include. I turn that into a short, sweet, funny video that feels personal and easy to share.

This shop is intentionally simple: one custom video, one easy form, digital delivery through Etsy messages, and one small revision included.

## Shop Policies

### Delivery

Videos are delivered digitally through Etsy messages within 48-72 hours after I receive the completed order form and usable dog photos.

### Revisions

One small revision is included. This covers a typo, name correction, pronoun correction, caption fix, or tiny wording adjustment.

### Cancellations

Because this is a custom digital product, cancellations are accepted only before video creation has started.

### Returns And Exchanges

This is a digital custom product, so returns and exchanges are not accepted after delivery. If there is an issue with your order, please message me and I will do my best to help.

### Photo Permission

By ordering, you confirm that you have permission to use the uploaded pet photos.

### Content Limits

Requests containing hateful, illegal, explicit, or inappropriate content may be declined.

## FAQ

### How long is the video?

Most videos are 30-45 seconds long.

### How do I send photos?

Upload 1-3 clear dog photos with your order, or send them through Etsy messages right after checkout.

### What kind of photo works best?

A clear photo where the dog is facing the camera works best. Good lighting and a high-resolution image help a lot.

### Can I order this as a gift?

Yes. These are made to be easy digital gifts. You can download the finished video and send it to the recipient.

### Can the dog say anything?

You can share the message you want included, as long as it is kind, legal, and appropriate. I may lightly edit wording so the video sounds natural.

### Is one revision included?

Yes. One small revision is included for things like a name correction, pronoun correction, typo, caption fix, or tiny wording change.

### Can you make a video with more than one dog?

For the $4.99 launch offer, please use one dog per video. A multi-dog option can be added later.

### Is this a physical product?

No. This is a digital video delivered through Etsy messages.
