# Simple Shop Brand Direction

## Recommended Shop Name

PupNoteVideos

Backup options:

- FromTheDogStudio
- WagMessageCo
- DearDogMomStudio
- TheTalkingPupGift

## Brand Feel

Light, friendly, warm, and easy. The shop should feel like a cute gift helper, not a tech product.

## Colors

- Warm cream: `#FFF7EB`
- Soft yellow: `#FFD166`
- Friendly coral: `#F77F6A`
- Soft teal: `#83C5BE`
- Gentle green: `#B7E4C7`
- Dark text: `#2B2118`

## Voice

Use plain, cheerful copy:

- "Send me your dog's photo and a few details."
- "I will turn it into a sweet, funny video gift."
- "Perfect for dog moms, birthdays, thank-yous, and just-because smiles."

Avoid:

- Technical AI language
- Overexplaining how the video is made
- Premium or luxury wording
- Anything that makes the buyer feel like they need to understand the technology
