# TalkingPetGiftCo Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 16, 2026

TalkingPetGiftCo uses Pinterest API access only to publish pins to the TalkingPetGiftCo Pinterest account.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information.

The app may use Pinterest account authorization, board information, pin images, pin titles, pin descriptions, and destination links only for the purpose of creating and managing Pinterest posts requested by the account owner.

Customer pet photos and order details are used only to fulfill custom digital video orders and related marketing posts approved by the shop owner.

The app is for internal business use by TalkingPetGiftCo. It does not provide public login or third-party user access.

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact TalkingPetGiftCo through the Etsy shop contact form:

https://www.etsy.com/shop/TalkingPetGiftCo
