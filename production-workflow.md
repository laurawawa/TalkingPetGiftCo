# Production Workflow

Goal: keep each $4.99 launch order under 30 minutes while still making it feel personal and giftable.

## New Order Checklist

1. Confirm the buyer submitted the form.
2. Confirm 1-3 dog photos were uploaded or sent through Etsy messages.
3. Check that the request is allowed and appropriate.
4. Pick the closest script template from `script-templates.md`.
5. Rewrite the script using the buyer's details.
6. Create the talking dog video.
7. Add captions if the audio or message needs clarity.
8. Export the video.
9. Send through Etsy messages with the delivery note below.
10. Log the order in `launch-tracker.csv`.

## Delivery Message

Hi! Your custom talking dog video is ready. I hope it brings a big smile.

One small revision is included, so if you notice a name pronunciation issue or a tiny wording change, just send it over and I will take care of it.

Thank you so much for supporting my new shop.

## Revision Rules

Included:

- Fixing a name or pronoun
- Small wording change
- Re-exporting with a corrected caption

Not included:

- A completely new script
- A different occasion
- Rebuilding the video with a new dog photo after delivery
- Multiple new style directions

## Quality Check

Before sending, confirm:

- Recipient name is correct
- Dog name is correct
- Pronouns are correct
- Occasion is correct
- Video is 30-45 seconds when possible
- Audio is understandable
- File opens correctly

## Weekly Capacity

Launch capacity is 10 orders per week.

If orders exceed capacity, increase processing time before raising the price. After the first 20 completed orders and 5 positive reviews, consider increasing the base price to $9.99-$14.99.
